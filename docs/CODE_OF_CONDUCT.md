# Veyora collaboration conduct

Veyora aims for calm, clear, respectful technical collaboration. This policy
applies to project-controlled review and collaboration spaces when those spaces
are authorized.

Participants must discuss work rather than personal attributes; give specific,
evidence-based feedback; respect privacy and consent; avoid harassment,
discrimination, threats, intimidation, sexualized conduct, doxxing, and
publication of private information; and stop behavior when a moderator asks.

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation to protect people and
the project. Enforcement should be proportionate, documented privately, and
share only the minimum information needed to act.

External contribution intake and public collaboration channels are currently
closed. No conduct-reporting contact is invented here. Before an external space
opens, an authorized steward must publish an accessible private reporting route,
response expectations, appeal path, privacy/retention terms, and responsible
moderators. Do not put a safety report or sensitive personal information in a
public issue.
