# UX copy review and comprehension study evidence

Records for the human copy reviews behind UX-ONB-001, UX-ONB-004, EXP-002,
EXP-003, and HELP-002. A copy review is performed by a reviewer who was not
the author of the copy and who checks it against the PRD's terminology,
plain-language, and honesty rules (section 6.1 and principle 2).
