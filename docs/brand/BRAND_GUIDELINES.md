# Veyora brand guidelines

These guidelines protect a calm, private, modern, and reliable product identity
while keeping the current asset status honest. They do not replace
[TRADEMARK.md](../legal/TRADEMARK.md), grant a commercial license, or limit rights provided
by applicable law.

## Current asset status

Veyora currently uses a text-only `Veyora` wordmark. There is no approved Veyora logo or icon.
No shield, keyhole, fingerprint, monogram, mascot, or placeholder
graphic may be presented as an official Veyora asset. A future asset requires
recorded creator, source, date, rights, originality review, approved terms, and
digest before these guidelines can identify it as official.

## Wordmark usage

Use the spelling `Veyora` and pronunciation `vee-OR-uh`. Keep the wordmark clear,
legible, and separate from third-party identities. Do not distort it, combine it
with another mark, imply endorsement, or use it as the primary branding of an
unofficial product.

## Color

The approved direction uses warm-neutral and graphite surfaces with one
restrained teal accent. Exact production values come only from reviewed,
frontend-owned design tokens. This document does not invent color values or
authorize a color combination as a standalone mark.

## Typography

The approved direction uses precise editorial typography. IBM Plex Sans and IBM Plex Mono are third-party font candidates, not Veyora-owned assets.
They may ship only after exact files, version, digest, license, notices, reserved
font-name status, modification state, and script coverage pass the third-party
inventory. Production fallbacks must cover every released writing system.

## Marketing and product materials

Official release, website, screenshot, tutorial, and marketing material must use
the evidence-bounded product language in [README.md](../../README.md), identify the
exact released version and capability state, and avoid fear, guarantees,
certification-like badges, or implied partnership. Apple, Proton, 1Password, and
Notion are mood references only; their assets, wording, layouts, interactions,
silhouettes, and trade dress must not be copied.

## Asset restrictions

No official Veyora asset may be modified, resold, or used to identify a
commercial or unofficial product unless a future asset-specific license expressly
permits that use. Because no logo or icon is approved today, this rule grants no
permission and claims no ownership in an uncreated asset. Accurate reference,
commentary, review, and other applicable-law uses remain unaffected.
