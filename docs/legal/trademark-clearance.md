no claim that `Veyora`, `vee-OR-uh`, the typographic wordmark, or any future asset
is registered, available, exclusive, or non-infringing. The release uses no `®`,
and no logo or icon has been approved.

trademark filing, or commercial licensing, an authorized owner or counsel must
record a dated comprehensive search and decision covering:

- `Veyora`, `vee-OR-uh`, confusing variants, sound, appearance, meaning, and
  commercial impression;
- relevant software, password/credential management, identity/private-data,
  hosted-service, and security/privacy goods and services;
- intended territories, federal and state sources, business registries,
  domains, international sources, app/package namespaces, and common-law use;
- actual owner, first-use basis, goods/services, risk disposition, and expiry or
  review date; and

Repository names, domains, package availability, or automated searches are not
clearance evidence. Until the gate closes, publication and trademark claims stay
blocked and no contact or fictional rights-holder is invented.
