This policy is separate from the source-code license. It governs use of the
Veyora name and current text-only wordmark as source identifiers. It does not
change rights granted by [LICENSE](LICENSE), applicable law, or fair and
nominative use.

## Ownership and current status

The Veyora Project uses and claims `Veyora` as the unregistered
source-identifying name for this project and its software. Any rights asserted
are limited to actual use and applicable law. No registration, worldwide exclusivity, or approved logo or icon is claimed.
This release does not represent that `Veyora`, `vee-OR-uh`, the wordmark, a
product name, or a visual asset is cleared or available in every jurisdiction.
The `®` symbol is not used.

The only current mark is the typographic `Veyora` wordmark. No logo or icon has
been approved. The separate [brand guidelines](BRAND_GUIDELINES.md) describe the
current visual system without creating rights in an unapproved asset.

## Allowed references

Accurate references, links, tutorials, and reviews are permitted when they do not imply sponsorship or endorsement.

Permitted factual uses include:

- refer accurately to Veyora and link to its authorized repository;
- discuss, review, teach, or write tutorials about Veyora;
- preserve required legal, provenance, compatibility, and historical references;
  and
- describe a qualifying modified distribution factually as “based on Veyora.”

## Restricted representations

Do not:

- present an unofficial fork, service, image, package, signature, or release as
  official, approved, supported, partnered with, sponsored, or endorsed by
  Veyora;
- use `Veyora` as the primary identity of a modified public product, service,
  domain, namespace, package, or app listing in a way likely to create source
  confusion;
- use a Veyora wordmark or future approved asset to identify an unofficial
  product; or
- remove required notices or falsify origin, compatibility, or release status.

## Fork rules

Forking and noncommercial modification remain subject to [LICENSE](LICENSE).
Renamed noncommercial forks may be distributed or hosted to the extent permitted by LICENSE when they are accurately attributed and do not imply sponsorship or endorsement.
Modified public versions must use a different primary name and branding and must clearly state that they are unofficial and modified.
They must also preserve required notices and provenance. `ExampleVault, based on
Veyora` is an acceptable factual pattern when accurate and non-confusing.
`Veyora Pro by another company` is not an acceptable primary product identity.

## Clearance and commercial status

Before the Veyora Project presents source, packages, hosted operation, or an
app-store listing as an official Veyora-branded offering; files a trademark;
authorizes a confusing Veyora-primary identity; or offers commercial licensing,
an authorized owner or counsel must record a dated clearance review for `Veyora`
and `vee-OR-uh` across the relevant goods, services, territories, and similar
marks. This internal official-brand gate does not restrict accurately attributed,
renamed noncommercial fork distribution or hosting to the extent permitted by
[LICENSE](LICENSE) and applicable law. No clearance or commercial permission is
offered through this release, and no enforcement or commercial-contact channel
is published.
